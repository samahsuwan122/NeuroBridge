Help & guidance

Common questions,
clear boundaries.

A concise view of how NeuroBridge supports care while keeping review, access, and responsibility clear.

What does NeuroBridge do?

NeuroBridge connects patients, families, and care teams to organize daily activities, appointments, messages, and performance-only progress summaries.

What is the scope of NeuroBridge?

NeuroBridge supports daily activities, performance-only progress, supportive follow-up, and care-team review.

Can families add memories?

Yes. Linked families can add photos, stories, and encouragement within scoped access.

Who reviews supportive suggestions?

Supportive suggestions remain pending care-team review.

Can clinics request a demo?

Yes. Clinics can submit a request through the Request demo page.
